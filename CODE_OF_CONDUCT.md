# Code of conduct

ReLand contributors must communicate respectfully and focus criticism on the
work, not the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, publishing another person's private data, or deliberately unsafe
security disclosure.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainers rather than escalating them in public threads.
